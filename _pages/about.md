**Contact :** raphael.tran-thanh [at] telecom-paris.fr

I am currently a PhD student at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/) (Institut Polytechnique de Paris), working with [Laurent Decreusefond](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/laurent-decreusefond) and [Nicolas Privault](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/nprivault/) in the Information Processing and Communications Laboratory (LTCI). <!--I am enrolled in an international 'cotutelle' agreement with [Nanyang Technological University - NTU Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/).-->

My research focuses on stochastic processes in relation with diffusion generative models. I am interested in the theory of Schrödinger bridges on general topological spaces.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a Master's degree in Applied Mathematics at Institut Polytechnique de Paris, pursuing the [Mathematics for Life Sciences](https://sites.google.com/view/m2-msv/accueil) track supervised by Sylvie Méléard. I was also a Master's student at Télécom Paris, where I specialized in Stochastic Modeling and Signal Processing for Artificial Intelligence. 

Feel free to reach out to me, or find my CV [here](/files/CV_recherche_TRAN-THANH_Raphaël_EN.pdf).
