**Abstract.** In this paper, we describe the dynamics of the biparental Moran model under the input of a small fraction of genetically advantaged individuals. We derive the convergence of the discrete model to the solution of a dynamical system describing the evolution of the advantaged individuals and their genetic weight among the whole population.
